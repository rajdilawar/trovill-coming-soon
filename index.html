Frequently Asked Questions

What is Trovill?

Trovill is a Berlin-based fashion brand creating modern Mediterranean-style menswear with conscious craftsmanship, carefully selected materials, and timeless design. Launching in early 2027.

When does Trovill launch?

Trovill launches in early 2027. Join our waitlist for exclusive early access to the first collection.

Where is Trovill based?

Trovill is based in Berlin, Germany, and creates modern Mediterranean-style apparel for the European market and beyond.

What is Trovill's approach to fashion?

Trovill is committed to conscious craftsmanship using carefully selected, honest materials. We focus on creating quality clothing that endures over time, opposing fast fashion culture.
