Our Story

Since 2009 · Berlin

TROVILL began with a dream in 2009, long before it became a registered brand in Berlin in 2026.

Growing up in a family involved in the clothing business, our founder developed an early love for fabrics, colours, and design. Over time, that interest became an ambition: to create a clothing brand of her own.

Her work as a Senior Designer and Merchandiser at a fashion boutique helped her build the skills to pursue it. Working closely with garments deepened her appreciation for fabric, fit, and the small details that make a piece feel right.

When she moved to Berlin, the idea she had carried for years began to take shape. TROVILL grew from that experience, bringing together her background in clothing and her love of Mediterranean style.

Our name draws from two places: Trogir in Croatia and Seville in Spain. Trogir’s coastal charm and historic streets, together with Seville’s warm colours and rich architectural influences, inspired the spirit of the brand. Their mix of traditions and styles reflects something we value in design: different elements coming together while retaining their character.

We carry that inspiration into our menswear through considered combinations of colour, texture, and fabric. We want to create clothes with personality that feel comfortable, fit naturally into your wardrobe, and remain a pleasure to wear.

Each design involves choices we care about—from how a fabric feels against the skin to how a garment sits on the body. We take time over those details because they shape your experience of wearing it.

TROVILL is a long-held dream becoming something you can touch, wear, and make your own. As we build the brand, our aim remains simple: to make thoughtful clothing that earns a lasting place in your wardrobe.
